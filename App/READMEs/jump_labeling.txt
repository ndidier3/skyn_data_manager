TAC JUMP LABELING PROCESSING
============================

OVERVIEW:
Identifies sudden increases (jumps) in TAC values that represent potential artifacts 
or sensor errors, and labels consecutive readings that continue to exceed realistic 
TAC bounds based on the initial jump magnitude.

PSEUDOCODE:
===========

For each reading (n) in a dataset:
    Identify max TAC between TACn-60 and TACn+60 (centered 2-hour window)
    
    If (TACn+1 - TACn) > 100 μg/L, OR
    If (TACn+1 - TACn) > (0.9 × Local Peak) AND Local Peak ≥ 40 μg/L:
        Label reading n and n+1 as "jump"
        Set Projected TAC = TACn
        
        For each subsequent reading (n+x), starting with x=2:
            If TACn+x < Projected TAC OR x > 360:
                STOP labeling
            Else:
                Label reading n+x as "jump"
                Adjust Projected TAC by +1.0 μg/L

DETECTION CRITERIA:
==================
- Absolute threshold: TAC increase > 100 μg/L between consecutive readings
- Relative threshold: TAC increase > 90% of max TAC in 2-hour window (when max ≥ 40 μg/L)

PROJECTION MECHANISM:
====================
- Creates an increasing upper bound starting at the initial TAC value
- Increments bound by +1.0 μg/L each time a reading is labeled as jump
- Stops when TAC readings fall below the projected bound

STOPPING CONDITIONS:
===================
1. TACn+x < Projected TAC (reading fall within a realistic TAC range)
2. x > 360 (maximum projection length of 6 hours reached)
