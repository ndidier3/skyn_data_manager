TAC JUMP LABELING PROCESSING
============================

OVERVIEW:
Identifies sudden increases (jumps) in TAC values that represent potential artifacts 
or sensor errors, and labels consecutive readings that continue to exceed realistic 
TAC bounds based on the initial jump magnitude.

PSEUDOCODE:
===========

For each reading (n) in a dataset:
    Identify max TAC between TACn-60 and TACn+60 (centered 2-hour window)
    Build a 10-minute forward window (up to TACn+10) while ignoring NaNs
    
    If (TACn+1 - TACn) > 100 μg/L, OR
    If (TACn+1 - TACn) > (0.9 × Local Peak) AND Local Peak ≥ 40 μg/L, OR
    If 10-minute window meets ALL of the following:
        • Contains ≥2 non-NaN readings
        • Shares its maximum value with the hour window (to the nearest 0.1 μg/L)
        • Average rise rate from TACn to that local peak > 30 μg/L per minute
        • Hour-window minimum after the peak is at least two-thirds closer to the 10-minute minimum than its maximum
      Then:
        Label readings from n through the 10-minute local peak as "jump"
        Set Projected TAC = TAC at that peak
    Else (for the first two conditions above):
        Label reading n and n+1 as "jump"
        Set Projected TAC = TACn
    
    For each subsequent reading (n+x), starting immediately after the last labeled index:
        If TACn+x < Projected TAC OR x > 360:
            STOP labeling
        Else:
            Label reading n+x as "jump"
            Adjust Projected TAC by +1.0 μg/L

DETECTION CRITERIA:
==================
- Absolute threshold: TAC increase > 100 μg/L between consecutive readings
- Relative threshold: TAC increase > 90% of max TAC in 2-hour window (when max ≥ 40 μg/L)
- Multi-minute surge: 10-minute window peak that matches the hour-window peak, rises > 30 μg/L per minute from the current value, and is followed by a pronounced post-peak drop (hour-window minimum after peak biased ≥ two-thirds toward the 10-minute minimum). Window tolerates NaNs provided at least two data points remain.

PROJECTION MECHANISM:
====================
- Creates an increasing upper bound starting at the initial TAC value
- Increments bound by +1.0 μg/L each time a reading is labeled as jump
- Stops when TAC readings fall below the projected bound

STOPPING CONDITIONS:
===================
1. TACn+x < Projected TAC (reading fall within a realistic TAC range)
2. x > 360 (maximum projection length of 6 hours reached)
