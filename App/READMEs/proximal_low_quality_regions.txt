PROXIMAL LOW QUALITY REGION PROCESSING
=====================================

OVERVIEW:
Creates buffer zones around low quality regions and merges nearby regions to ensure 
comprehensive coverage of areas that may be affected by data quality issues, while 
maintaining temporal relationships between adjacent problematic regions. A low-quality 
region consists of a combined, consecutive set of gaps, non-wear, jumps, plummets, 
and extreme negatives.

PSEUDOCODE:
===========

For each low-quality region:
    Calculate region_length = (end_n - start_n + 1)
    Determine extension_length = min(15, max(3, 3 + round(region_length/7)))
        - Minimum extension: 3 positions
        - Maximum extension: 15 positions  
        - Scales linearly: ~90 minutes required to reach max extension of 15
    Label readings as proximal low-quality:
        - From (start_n - extension_length) to start_n
        - From end_n to (end_n + extension_length)

For each pair of adjacent low-quality regions:
    Calculate combined_length = (region1_length + region2_length)
    Calculate region_distance = (region1_end_n - region2_start_n)
    
    Determine merge_distance:
        If combined_length ≤ 10: merge_distance = 10
        If combined_length ≥ 60: merge_distance = 20
        Else: merge_distance = 10 + ((combined_length - 10) × (10/50))
    
    If region_distance ≤ merge_distance:
        Label readings between low-quality regions as proximal low-quality, producing a single extended low-quality region
