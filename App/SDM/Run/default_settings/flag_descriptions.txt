Flag Descriptions
================

Region Flags (using both percent and duration)
-------------------------------------------
1. Unimputed Gaps/Non-wear: >20% OR >2hrs

Curve Flags (using both percent and duration)
------------------------------------------
1. Unimputed Low Quality: >5% OR >30min
2. Imputed Low Quality: >30% OR >3hrs
3. Below Threshold: >50%

Periphery Flags (using only percent)
---------------------------------
1. Gaps/Non-wear: >40%
2. Extreme Negative: >40%
3. Low Quality: >50%
4. Unimputed Low Quality: >20%

Rise/Fall Flags (using only percent)
--------------------------------
1. Incomplete Start or Fall: <50%
2. Low Quality Rise: >50%
3. Incomplete End: <50%
4. Low Quality Fall: >50%

Note: All percentages are decimals (0.50 = 50%)
